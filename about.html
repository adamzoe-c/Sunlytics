The Person Behind Sunlytics

Founder & Developer

Sunlytics was founded by a self-taught developer with a background in healthcare, someone who understands both data-driven decision making and the importance of clear, reliable information.

After rebuilding a career through technology following personal hardship, the mission became clear: use modern tools to solve real problems for real people. Discovering that solar assessment in Lithuania still required weeks of waiting and expensive engineer visits sparked the idea that became Sunlytics.

Today, Sunlytics combines satellite imagery, AI analysis, and certified drone operators to deliver in seconds and 24 hours what used to take weeks.

Our Story

Sunlytics was born from a simple frustration: discovering that solar assessment in Lithuania still required weeks of waiting and expensive engineer visits, when technology could do it in seconds. After researching the Lithuanian solar market, we identified a clear gap and built the solution.

How We Work Today

What started as a one-person idea now runs as a full pipeline. Sunlytics works with a growing network of LCAA-certified drone pilots across Lithuania, an in-house AI analysis system, and certified installation partners who turn every report into a working solar system on your roof.

Our Mission

To make solar energy accessible and understandable for every property owner in Lithuania by replacing slow, expensive assessments with instant satellite analysis and professional drone reports delivered in 24 hours.
