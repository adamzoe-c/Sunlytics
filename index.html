Why commercial roofs are a stronger case for solar
A warehouse or production facility mostly consumes electricity during the day, exactly when a solar system produces the most. That means less energy exported to the grid at a lower rate, and a payback period that's often significantly shorter than residential. Before committing to installers or financing, an independent survey gives your team real roof data, obstruction mapping, and a production estimate to work from.
