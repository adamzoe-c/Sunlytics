What's Included
Every survey package comes fully equipped
We do the assessment. You do the installation. Walk in with real roof data already in hand, not a cold introduction.

Drone Roof Survey

High-resolution imagery of the actual roof captured by a certified drone operator, scaled for large industrial and commercial roofs. No satellite approximations, real footage from the day of the mission.

Exact Roof Measurements

Usable roof area in m², pitch, orientation, and an obstruction map covering HVAC units, skylights and walkways. The measurements your team would normally spend hours calculating on a large roof are already done.

Solar Score & kWp Estimate

Our AI calculates a recommended commercial-scale system size (kWp), estimated annual production (kWh/yr), and a payback projection, ready to present directly to the decision-maker.

Professional PDF Report

A branded, printable report you can hand to the facility manager or property owner at your first meeting. It signals professionalism before you say a word.

A Verified Opportunity

The property has already been surveyed and screened for solar viability: roof condition, usable area, and structural fit are confirmed before it ever reaches you. Not a cold guess, a qualified opportunity.

Fast Turnaround

From the completed drone survey to the moment you receive the full package, our standard turnaround is a few business days, even for large industrial roofs. You can follow up while interest is still fresh.

How it works for you
From identifying the opportunity to your installation quote, in three steps.
1

We Identify & Commission the Survey
